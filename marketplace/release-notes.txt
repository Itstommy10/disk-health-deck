First public release.

• Nine actions: Disk Space, Disk Health, Disk Temperature, Disk Activity, Disk Overview, Network, Fan, Power and Back.
• SMART health: remaining life, power-on hours and start count, matched to each drive letter by model, sensor name or closest match.
• Threshold alerts on free space, temperature and remaining life, with a flashing frame.
• Automatic rotation through every drive or sensor, with press to advance.
• Full-screen dashboard profiles for Stream Deck, XL, Mini and Stream Deck +.
• Sensors discovered automatically from LibreHardwareMonitor (any local address and port) with fallback to HWiNFO.
• English and Italian, following the Stream Deck app language.
